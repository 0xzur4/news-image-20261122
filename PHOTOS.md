Photos are illustrative of their stories (file photos of the places and
people, not taken at the 22 November events): the COP31 protest photo is
from the Antalya summit, the Manama skyline stands for the Bahrain strike,
the Metro Bridge photo for the strikes around Kyiv, the Mercury image is
NASA's colour mosaic of the planet, the Peace Palace photo stands for the
World Court in The Hague, and the Bitcoin photo is a file photo of
physical bitcoins. Two of the files — the Kyiv bridge and the Bitcoin
coins — are the same Commons files used for the 20 November entry, kept
because they are the clearest file photos of those subjects; the other
four were downloaded fresh for this entry.
